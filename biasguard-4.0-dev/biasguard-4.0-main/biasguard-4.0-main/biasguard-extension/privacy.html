BiasGuard Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

1. Introduction

BiasGuard ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the BiasGuard Chrome Extension.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local Storage

BiasGuard uses Chrome's local storage to save:

  • Your extension settings and preferences
  • Usage statistics (anonymously, local only)
  • Cached analysis results (5-minute TTL)
  • Session information

2.2 Cookies

With your consent, we may use cookies for:

  • Necessary Cookies: Required for the extension to function
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand usage patterns (anonymous)
  • Marketing Cookies: Track campaign effectiveness (if enabled)

2.3 Text Analysis

When you use BiasGuard to analyze text, the text is sent to your configured API endpoint (default: localhost:3001). We do not store or transmit this text to any third-party services unless explicitly configured by you.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide bias detection and analysis services
  • To improve the extension's functionality
  • To remember your preferences and settings
  • To generate anonymous usage statistics

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage APIs. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to external servers unless:

  • You explicitly configure a custom API endpoint
  • You choose to share analysis results

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your stored data through Chrome's storage settings
  • Delete your data at any time
  • Modify your cookie preferences
  • Disable the extension at any time

6. Third-Party Services

BiasGuard may connect to external APIs for text analysis. By default, this is your local API server. If you configure a different endpoint, that service's privacy policy applies.

7. Children's Privacy

BiasGuard is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date.
